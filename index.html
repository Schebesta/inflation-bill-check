The ad creative under test

Three square (1080×1080) variants pointing at this page. Each one tests a different entry angle: the number, the adjacent category, and the plain prompt. Same destination, same three modules.

What this test is actually asking

This is an Opportunity Sniper draft: a timely macro signal turned into a specific, finishable action across three existing Finder destinations.

The signal

An inflation print is a dated, news-pegged reason to act. It gives the ad a why-today that an evergreen "save money" message never has.

The reframe

Most cost-of-living advice targets discretionary spending. Recurring bills are larger, repriced without warning, and switchable in minutes.

The constraint

Exactly three bills and one time estimate. A bounded list is finishable; an open-ended audit gets postponed.

The bet

A specific numeric hook plus a bounded checklist beats generic savings messaging on click-through and on downstream intent, not just clicks.
