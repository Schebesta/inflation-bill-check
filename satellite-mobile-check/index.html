What to check before changing plans

Satellite-to-mobile is not just a cheaper-data story. It changes the comparison question: when you lose normal mobile coverage, does your plan give you a useful fallback?

Feature limits

Check whether the service supports text only, emergency messaging, voice, data or all of the above.

Phone support

Look for the exact compatible devices and software versions, not just the provider's brand claim.

Coverage use case

If you rarely leave metro coverage, a cheaper plan may beat paying for a feature you barely use.
