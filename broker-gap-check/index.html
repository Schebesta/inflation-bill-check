Compare home loans What to ask

Estimate only. Actual repayments depend on fees, loan term, repayment type and features.

What to ask your broker

A costlier loan can still be in your best interests. The useful question is whether the reason is clear, current and specific to you.

Show me the cheaper options

Ask for the comparable loans considered, including rates, fees and exclusions.

Explain the trade-off

Offset, redraw, cashback and approval certainty can matter, but the benefit should be named.

Check again before signing

Rates move quickly. Run one fresh comparison before settlement or refinance approval.
