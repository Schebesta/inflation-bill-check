Three numbers to check

This is the campaign flow: make the risk visible, then move people to a loan health check before the next repricing cycle.

1

What did you pay?

Start with the contract price and deposit size, not the lender's original valuation.

2

What is it worth now?

Use a suburb-level estimate, then stress test the reported 5.2% national fall.

3

What LVR band are you in?

Flag borrowers who moved above 80%, 90%, or into thin-equity territory.

Landing-page brief

  • Input: purchase price, deposit, current loan balance, postcode/suburb.
  • Output: estimated equity buffer, current LVR, and "ask your lender" script.
  • Primary CTA: compare refinance options by LVR band.
  • Secondary CTA: set a reminder for the 13 Oct RBA minutes and 3 Nov decision.
