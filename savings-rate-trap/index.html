The Finder flow

Make the gap between the advertised rate and the earned rate concrete, then route savers to a better account or to the conditions they are quietly failing.

1

Separate the two rates

Ask for the headline bonus rate and the base rate that applies when a condition is missed.

2

Count the missed months

Most savers cannot name their conditions, let alone how often they broke them. Make them count.

3

Route on the gap

Big gap and frequent misses means switch. Big gap and rare misses means fix the conditions.

Landing-page brief

  • Input: savings balance, headline bonus rate, base/fallback rate, and months the conditions were missed.
  • Output: dollars lost over 12 months, interest earned versus interest advertised, the rate actually earned, and the cost of a single slip.
  • Primary CTA: compare savings accounts, weighted to accounts with a liveable base rate rather than the biggest headline.
  • Secondary CTA: the bonus-condition check, for savers whose account is fine but whose habits are not.
  • Timing peg: the +0.25% variable home-loan rises land 9 Oct 2026, so the asymmetry story is live that week.
