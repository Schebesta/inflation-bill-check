What to do with your result

  • If payback is under 6 months, compare refinance loans and check eligibility.
  • If payback is 6-12 months, compare loans and ask your current lender for a sharper rate.
  • If payback is over 12 months, the switch may still be useful, but the upfront cost needs more scrutiny.
  • Check the comparison rate, fees, cashback conditions, loan features and total repayment before applying.
  • Use this as a guide only. A lender's actual offer depends on your circumstances and credit approval.

Important information. This calculator provides a simple estimate only. It is factual information, not personal advice, and does not consider your objectives, financial situation or needs.

Results are not guaranteed. Actual savings depend on your loan balance, rate, fees, loan term, credit approval, lender criteria and product features. Before switching, read the relevant terms and consider whether the product is right for you.
