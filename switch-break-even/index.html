Landing-page brief

  • Input: balance, current rate, possible new rate and total switching cost.
  • Output: monthly saving, annual saving, first-year net and break-even month.
  • Primary CTA: refinance comparison when payback is under 12 months.
  • Secondary CTA: retention-call script when payback is weak but the rate gap is real.
  • Timing peg: big four rate rises are now live and Thursday's jobs print decides the next RBA narrative.
